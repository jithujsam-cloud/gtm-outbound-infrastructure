# gtm-outbound-infrastructure

Production n8n systems built and used in live B2B outbound campaigns. Not templates, not tutorials. Built at MapmyIndia for enterprise accounts and for clients at Clospect, where these systems run against real target lists in D2C, fintech, pharma, defense, and semiconductor verticals.

## Systems

### [Signal-to-Pipeline](systems/signal-to-pipeline/) 🔍
LinkedIn job postings go in. ICP-scored, email-validated leads come out, ready for sequencing.

### [Outbound Engine](systems/outbound-engine/) ✉️
Qualified leads go in. AI-rewritten emails go out via Gmail, with open tracking wired back to Airtable.

## Stack

n8n, Apify, Gemini, Claude (Anthropic), Clearout, Airtable, Google Sheets, Gmail

## Background

At MapmyIndia I ran outbound targeting VP and C-suite in BFSI, fintech, and public sector. The bottleneck was never the call or the email, it was the research: figuring out which accounts to hit, whether the contact was real, and whether the email would actually land. That process was entirely manual and it ate 40-50% of working time. These systems automate the triage layer so the rep spends time on pipeline, not on spreadsheets. The job signal workflow came later, when it became clear that buying signals in job postings are more reliable than firmographic filters alone.
