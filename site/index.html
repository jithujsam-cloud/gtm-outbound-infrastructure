  1. idleWaiting for a lead.

The company and contact are invented for this demo. The seven steps are the real pipeline.

  1. 01Signal Apify

    Pull job postings by industry and role. A hiring spike is a buying signal.

  2. 02Score Gemini

    Match the account to the ICP and write down why. Every match or miss has a reason.

  3. 03Grade rules

    Email structure, name and domain match, no generic prefixes.

  4. 04Verify Clearout

    An SMTP check, only on leads that passed the first three.

  5. 05Queue Airtable

    Marked safe to send.

  6. 06Send Claude, Gmail

    Weekdays 9 to 6, four per run, random gaps, every email rewritten.

  7. 07Track pixel

    A self-hosted pixel stamps the open time. No third-party tracker.

How I work

  1. 01Choose the account before the message.Research the company, map the buyers and qualify fit first, so the message reaches the right function.
  2. 02Time it to a reason to act.Funding news, regulation, a traffic peak or a launch gives the buyer a reason to reply this month.
  3. 03Own the whole loop.Research, copy, outreach, qualification and handoff stay with one person, so nothing is lost between steps.
  4. 04Build a tool when the work repeats.A process done three times becomes a workflow. A workflow other people need becomes a product.
  5. 05Learn by shipping.That is how n8n, Claude Code and Klaroh joined the toolkit.
