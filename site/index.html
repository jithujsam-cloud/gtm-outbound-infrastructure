Klaroh

Client reporting for cold-email agencies.

The problem
Agencies running cold email for several clients struggle to keep an eye on each client’s campaign performance and sending health, and end up reporting with screenshots and PDFs.
What I built
A multi-tenant dashboard that syncs campaign data from Instantly, shows every client’s campaigns, replies and mailbox health in one place, and gives each client a private report that opens without a login. Any metric whose source can’t be verified is left out, not guessed.
What works today
Instantly is the one connected sending tool, data syncs on a schedule and on demand, and Klaroh never sends email.

GTM Validation Tool

Lead list validation that doesn’t pay twice.

The problem
Verifying every email in a raw lead list costs money, and long runs fail halfway through when a provider rate-limits or a request times out.
What I built
A Next.js and Supabase app that imports a CSV, scores each lead against the ICP with structured AI output, and only then verifies emails through Clearout, on a PostgreSQL job queue with leases, retries, rate-limit handling and cost logging.
What works today
Import, ICP scoring and email verification work end to end, and long jobs pause and resume safely.

Signal-to-pipeline workflows

Outbound that starts from a live hiring signal.

The problem
Lists exported from a database are weeks old by the time anyone emails them, and enriching every row burns budget on contacts that never fit.
What I built
Two n8n systems. One starts from hiring signals and runs the cheap checks before the paid one, storing every score with its written reason. The other sends on a controlled cadence and records opens with a self-hosted pixel.
What works today
Workflow files, not a hosted product. They need your own n8n instance and API keys.
Workflow files
  1. 1SignalApify pulls job postings
  2. 2ScoreGemini checks ICP fit and says why
  3. 3GradeEmail quality scored with reasons
  4. 4VerifyClearout, only for leads that passed
  5. 5QueueAirtable marks it safe to send
  6. 6SendClaude rewrites, Gmail sends with gaps
  7. 7TrackA self-hosted pixel records the open
A lead that fails step 2 never costs a verification credit
