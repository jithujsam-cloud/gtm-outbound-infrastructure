How it works

From job posting to opened email.

Seven steps, in order. The cheap checks run first, so no money goes to verifying a bad lead.

  1. 1

    Catch the signal · Apify

    Pull job postings by industry and role.

  2. 2

    Score the account · Gemini

    Match against the ICP, with a written reason.

  3. 3

    Grade the email · Points

    Structure, name, domain, no generic prefixes.

  4. 4

    Verify what passed · Clearout

    SMTP check only on qualified leads.

  5. 5

    Queue it · Airtable

    Marked Safe to Send.

  6. 6

    Send like a person · Claude, Gmail

    Weekdays 9–6, 4 per run, random gaps, rewritten every time.

  7. 7

    Track the open · Pixel

    A self-hosted pixel stamps the open time.
