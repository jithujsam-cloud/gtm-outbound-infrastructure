Ledgerline and its contact are made up. The seven steps are the real ones.

I joined MapmyIndia in 2022 to answer inbound leads. About six months in I moved to outbound, and for more than three years I was the only person generating outbound leads for enterprise sales.

Banks, NBFCs, fintechs, retailers, logistics firms and airports have all had a message from me. Each got a different use case for its sector, and a reason to reply this month.

I trained the team that now runs my playbook, and I taught myself n8n, Apify, Gemini and Claude Code by rebuilding my own workflow as software.

In January 2026 I started Clospect to run the same engine for other B2B companies. I also built Klaroh, a reporting tool for the agencies that run it.
