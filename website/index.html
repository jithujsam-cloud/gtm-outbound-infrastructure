What I do

GTM engineering, as I practise it, is four jobs held by one person.

Targeting
Define the ICP, map the buying committee, and pick accounts that have a reason to talk now: a regulation, a launch, a hiring spike.
Outreach
Write the positioning and the copy, run email, LinkedIn and calls, and keep the sending domains healthy enough to land in the inbox.
Systems
Turn work that repeats into workflows: enrichment, AI scoring, verification and sending, built in n8n and code, and sized to the budget.
Operations
Track every opportunity in the CRM, hand qualified accounts to sales, and train the people who run the playbook after me.

How I work

  • Choose the account before the message.Research the company, map the buyers and qualify fit first, so the message reaches the right function.
  • Time it to a reason to act.Funding news, regulation, a traffic peak or a launch gives the buyer a reason to reply this month.
  • Own the whole loop.Research, copy, outreach, qualification and handoff stay with one person, so nothing is lost between steps.
  • Build a tool when the work repeats.A process done three times becomes a workflow. A workflow other people need becomes a product.
  • Learn by shipping.I pick up a new tool by building something real with it, then fold the result back into the sales process.

Experience

Four years in GTM, told once.

I joined MapmyIndia in 2022 to answer inbound leads, moved to outbound within the year, and for more than three years was the only person generating outbound leads for enterprise sales. That meant selling location technology into banks, NBFCs, fintech, retail, logistics, gaming, automotive, public sector and real estate, each with its own use case and its own reason to reply.

Along the way the work moved from manual outreach to automated systems, and the team I trained now runs the playbook. In 2026 I started Clospect to build the same engine for other B2B companies.
